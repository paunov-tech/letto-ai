Frequently asked

How much does a flight to London from Belgrade cost?
Typical range €120–€350 in season. Letto surfaces deals below €120 when they appear (engine refreshes every 6h).
How long is the Belgrade–London flight?
About 2h 55min direct. With a connection it can be significantly longer — Letto shows both options.
When is the best season to visit London?
Best months are April, May, June, September. Price and weather align in this window.
How much is a hotel in London per night?
Hotels range from €100 (3★ budget) up to €300+ (5★ luxury) per night.
Do I need a visa for London?
UK ETA (electronic travel authorization) required from 2025 — quick online application, ~£10, valid for 2 years.
